Privacy Policy

App: Light Mix · Developer: J.Biglion · Effective date: November 6, 2026

J.Biglion (the “Developer”) respects the privacy of everyone who uses Light Mix (the “App”). This policy explains what information the App handles.

1. At a glance

  • The App does not ask you to sign up or sign in.
  • The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • The App does not request internet access and does not send information to any server of the Developer.
  • This version of the App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue your game, the following information is stored only on your device. The Developer cannot receive or view it.

InformationPurpose
Level completion records, current device placement and inventoryResuming play
Hint balance and per-level hint grant recordsProviding hints
Settings (large text, sound effects, vibration, reduced light effects)Keeping your preferences

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect or use account information, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or media files, microphone or camera input, or payment information. It also contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because the Developer does not collect personal information, the Developer does not share it with, or hand its processing to, any third party.

5. Device backup

If Android automatic backup is turned on, the operating system may back up the App’s stored data (game progress and settings) to your Google account. This backup takes place between you and Google, and the Developer cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device’s Android settings.

6. Retention and deletion

The stored information is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App’s data in your device settings. The Developer keeps no information separately.

7. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

8. When features such as advertising are added

If a new feature such as advertising changes the information the App handles, this policy will be updated and its effective date changed before that version is released. If advertising is introduced, this page will describe the information used by the ad service (for example, the advertising ID) and the choices available to you.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text and effective date will be posted on this page.
