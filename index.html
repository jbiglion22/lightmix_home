Mix

Light gets brighter as it mixes

Unlike paint, light adds up. Three beams are enough to make seven colors.

Red + GreenYellow
Green + BlueCyan
Red + BlueMagenta
Red + Green + BlueWhite

Exact or nothing. If the target is yellow and white arrives, the receiver stays off. Gather the light you need and steer the extra away.

How to play

Three steps to get it

The rules are simple. The puzzles get deeper with every chapter.

Place mirrors

Drag a mirror from the tray, or select it and tap an empty cell.

Turn them

Tap a mirror to flip between / and \ and watch the light path change instantly.

Light the receivers

When every receiver matches its target color exactly, you win. Mistakes are always undoable.

Tools

New tools keep arriving

Start with one mirror, and end up with combiners and filters. Every chapter brings a fresh idea.

Mirror

Bends light by 90 degrees. You can place, rotate, move and take it back.

Combiner

Gathers several beams into one and sends it out along its arrow.

Filter

Lets only one color component through, so you can strain out the excess.
